Shiyu / 拾语 - Windows WeChat chat export

1. Extract the entire ZIP to a writable folder, such as Documents.
   Do not run the EXE inside the ZIP preview.
2. Open Shiyu.exe. Keep the _internal folder beside it.
   Python, Git, and Microsoft Word are not required to export.
3. Choose your own xwechat_files folder and click 开始检测微信.
4. Keep Shiyu open. Fully exit WeChat from its menu, restart it, and log in.
   Closing the WeChat window alone may leave it running.
5. Once the key is captured, open an original chat image in WeChat,
   then click 读取聊天与图片.
6. Choose a conversation and click 导出 Word (.docx).

Archives and exports are saved in the data folder beside the app.
Keep your archives when upgrading. Never share data or your local.json.
Windows 10/11 x64. Live import tested with WeChat 4.1.13.12.
Other versions may need adaptation. The interface is currently Chinese.
This build is not digitally signed. See README.md for troubleshooting.

完整解压后打开 Shiyu.exe，不要单独移动 EXE。
首次使用选择自己的微信数据目录，再点击“开始检测微信”。
保持拾语开启，完全退出微信后重新启动并登录。
成功获取后打开聊天原图，再读取聊天与图片，选择对象导出。
